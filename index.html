What it does

Practice you can measure

Your shot timer gives you one number. Shot Timer Drills turns every string into splits, scores and trends, so you know what to work on next.

Live from your timer

Score strings in seconds

Tap Shooter Ready. The app arms your timer, catches each shot as it happens and stops at the stage's round count.

  • Time and every split appear automatically, so you don't type anything in
  • Hit factor, time plus and points scoring, with USPSA/IPSC, IDPA and custom zones
  • Steel hit/miss, penalties, par times and pass/fail

Video

Record every run

Film each string with your phone. The video syncs to your timer's start beep and burns your time and splits onto the footage.

  • Start beep detected automatically
  • See exactly which shot was slow
  • Share the video or a result card with anyone

Progress

See yourself improve

Every drill keeps its own history, so you can track your times and personal bests over weeks of practice.

  • Personal bests for every drill and stage
  • Trend charts and "where to focus" insights
  • Compare any two runs, shot by shot

Drill library

30 drills built in. Build your own.

Classics, standards and qualifications, from the Bill Drill and El Presidente to the FAST drill and the FBI qualification.

  • Each stage lists its rounds, distance, targets and par time
  • Build drills on one page, or import them from a spreadsheet
  • Live fire and dry fire, with par beeps from your phone

Built for range days

Plan range trips

Line up the drills you're bringing, then run them in order. Resume picks up where you left off.

Shoot with friends

Add shooters and run the full drill each or rotate stage by stage. Then check the standings.

Score from your wrist

Count hits and misses on Apple Watch after the string, without reaching for your phone.

Phone in your pocket

The timer connection stays alive with the screen locked, so you can put the phone away and shoot.

Jump back in

Your recent drills are one tap from running again, and you can record video while you're at it.

Your data stays yours

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your history stays on your phone, and you can export it anytime.
