Contact Us

Shot Timer Drills is built by an independent developer, and every message is read by the person who makes the app.

Email support@shottimerdrills.com

  • Help with the app

    Many common questions are answered on the Support page and in the How-To Guides. If yours isn't, email us and say whether you're on iPhone or Android.

  • Bug reports and feature requests

    In the app, Settings → Send Feedback opens your email app already addressed. A screenshot and a line about what you were doing help a lot.

  • Android beta

    To join the Android test on Google Play, request an invite here.

  • Shot timer makers and partnerships

    If you make a Bluetooth shot timer and want it supported, or have a partnership in mind, email us with "Partnership" in the subject.

  • Privacy and your data

    See the Privacy Policy for what the app stores. To have timer reports deleted, email us with your install ID.
