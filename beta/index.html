Android beta

Join the Android beta

Shot Timer Drills for Android is in testing on Google Play. Join in three taps from your Android phone, using the Google account you use for the Play Store.

Join in three taps

  1. Join the testers group.

    Tap Join group on the page that opens. Use the Google account that's on your Android phone.

    Join the group
  2. Become a tester.

    Tap Become a tester. If it says the test isn't available, give it a minute after joining the group and try again.

    Become a tester
  3. Install Shot Timer Drills from Google Play and connect your timer.

    Updates arrive through Google Play like any other app.

    Open in Google Play

Works with the Competition Electronics ProTimer BT, AMG Commander, SG Timer (Timer 2 and GO), DAA Edge and Kestrel KST1000. No timer yet? You can type times in by hand.

Rather we add you?

Leave the email of the Google account on your Android phone and we'll add you to the test and email you the link.

Your email is used only to invite you to the Android test and to reach you about it. It isn't shared or used for marketing.

You're on the list
